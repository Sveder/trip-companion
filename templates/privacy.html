Privacy

Trip Companion is for families. We keep as little as we can.

What we keep

  • Your email address. You sign in with it. We send you sign-in codes and, unless you stop them, a short reminder every two days.
  • Your trip. Where you go, the dates, and the age of each traveller. We do not ask for names.
  • Your course. Which activities you did, and your quiz answers, so the quiz can repeat the words you missed.
  • Videos you choose to save. Only your family can see them. They are not public and we never share them.
  • Usage counts. We count actions (for example "an activity was done") to see what works. We do not sell them or show them to anyone.
  • Error reports. When a page breaks, a report goes to Sentry, our error service. We do not send your email address with it.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or give your data to anyone.
  • Shop links (for example kids' chopsticks) are plain links. The shop sees your visit only if you open the link.

Delete your account

Email m@sveder.com from the address you sign in with. We delete your account, your trip, your course and your videos within 7 days, and we write back when it is done.

Stop the reminder emails

Every reminder has a "stop" link at the bottom. One tap stops them.
