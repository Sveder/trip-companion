# Trip Companion (working title)

A kid-friendly, adult-friendly app that gets a family ready and excited for a trip abroad.

## The Unique Kernel
Not one feature: "a lot of good ideas coming together into one package that is very accessible", child friendly and adult
friendly, that prepares you for the trip so "you want to be excited about it". Excitement first; knowledge comes with it.
Japan is the full country. Other countries (Uzbekistan first) appear as "coming soon" / beta-preview cards, "to show how a
relatively unknown country can be unlocked".

## Who It's For
A parent planning a trip abroad with small children (here: Michael, with a 4-year-old and a 2-year-old), and the children themselves.
Today they read guide books and blogs alone, and the kids arrive knowing nothing about the place.

## The Core Loop
The family opens the app (on the TV, a phone or a laptop), does today's short activity together, and plays a quiz or
practice round that repeats earlier words and facts. They come back the next day for the next step of their course.

## Inspiration & Identity
- Style: kid friendly, bright and game-like, like Matific (https://www.matific.com/).
- Place facts in the spirit of the Geography Now YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@GeographyNow).
- Mood: excitement and anticipation, "ready and pumped".

## Why This Matters to the Learner
A real family trip to Japan with two small boys. And: "to understand if a plan first approach is going to be good and interesting."

## What "Working" Looks Like
His demo video, in his words: opening the app on the TV and watching a course on the foods; opening a box with chopsticks and
"seeing how the chopsticks work guided by the app"; then the quiz; then "the UI of how to build it all — you click a few buttons,
choose a few settings and it automatically builds you something".
The "oh, that's cool" beat: a few settings in, a full trip-prep course for Japan out; Uzbekistan and others wait as previews.

## The POC Boundary
Michael's decisions, 2026-10-07:
- Japan only, in full, with "quite a lot of activities" (the activity list is decided in the PRD).
- Other countries, Uzbekistan first, shown as "coming soon" / beta-preview cards, not built out.
- The course builder: who travels (adults, children and ages), how many days -> a course of daily steps.
- Japan modules: food (watchable on a TV screen), language (a few words, with repetition across days),
  do and don't in public, safety (emergency numbers).
- Real-world guided activities, e.g. chopsticks with a real pair.
- A quiz / practice round that repeats earlier items.
- Moved into "now" by Michael (2026-10-07): local transportation; a guided Street View tour; a buy-in-advance list
  (things to get before the trip, e.g. practice chopsticks); after-the-trip highlights.
- Kid-friendly UI that works on a phone and in the TV's web browser.
